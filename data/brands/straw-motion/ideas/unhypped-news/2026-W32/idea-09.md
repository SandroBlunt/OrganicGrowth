# idea-09 — "Three in four support issues now close without a human at BBVA and SoftBank"

## Angle
Two receipts landed instead of two promises. OpenAI launched Presence, an enterprise agent platform for
chat and voice, and early customers BBVA, SoftBank and IAG report 75% of issues resolved without human
intervention. Cursor shipped Router, which cuts AI coding costs 30-50% by sending each request to the
right model while holding or improving code quality. The tension: the public agent conversation is about
raw capability, and both of this week's wins came from unglamorous plumbing, a resolution rate and a
routing decision. Neither needed a smarter model.

## Hook concept
Open on 75%: at a bank (BBVA) and at SoftBank, three of every four support issues now close without a
person touching them. The surprise is that the headline agent number of the week is an operations metric,
not a benchmark. (Concept only, writer lands the final line.)

## Suggested Recipe
News Carousel (7 slides). Two case numbers plus a "here is the same lever at your size" turn is a clean
swipe, and the 75% figure makes a strong stat callout on card one.

## Talking points
- OpenAI launched Presence, an enterprise AI agent platform covering chat and voice; early adopters BBVA,
  SoftBank and IAG report 75% issue resolution without human intervention (OpenAI's launch post).
- That is a resolution rate, not a demo. For support work, the number that matters is how many tickets
  never reach a person at all.
- Cursor Router cuts AI coding costs 30-50% by routing each request to an appropriate model while
  maintaining or improving code quality (Cursor's own post).
- The pattern across both: this week's agent wins came from routing and workflow design, not from a
  better model.
- The same two levers work at any size. Send easy work to a cheap model, and measure "closed without me"
  instead of "sounded impressive."
- Un-hyped catch: 75% is a company's own reported figure under its own definition of resolved, and the
  remaining 25% is exactly the hard part, the angry, the ambiguous and the expensive.
