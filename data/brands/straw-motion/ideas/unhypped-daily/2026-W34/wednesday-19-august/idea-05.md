## The Catch (mandatory skeptic beat)
The entire story rests on an unreleased demo video found in someone else's software. No price, no date, no confirmation, no product. Apple routinely builds things it never sells, and every leak cycle produces features that quietly disappear. Treat this as a direction the company is exploring, not a thing you will be able to buy. And the privacy question does not go away because one article says it might be handled: an unconfirmed limit in an unannounced product is not a guarantee.
