## Angle
The tension is between how fast the number moved and how narrow the product is. Etched's valuation doubled to $21 billion inside a single month, and Etched builds a chip specialised for one model architecture rather than a general-purpose one. Its own post is titled "From Zero to One". The contrast to ride: the most concentrated bet in AI hardware is getting the least patient price, and nothing in the public evidence explains what changed about the company in those thirty days.

## Talking Points
- TechCrunch's headline states the exact move: Etched's "valuation doubles to $21B in a month" (Aug 18). A doubling inside thirty days is the fact here, not the framing.
- Etched published its own account under the title "From Zero to One" (etched.com/progress/from-zero-to-one), so there is a first-party page to put on screen alongside the coverage.
- Plain English for a beginner: general chips like Nvidia's GPUs will run any kind of AI. Etched's pitch is a chip built for one architecture only, which is how you get much faster, and also how you get stranded if the architecture changes.
- That trade-off is the investment case and the risk in the same sentence. It is genuinely unusual to find a company where the bull argument and the bear argument are the identical fact.
- The unhypped question the trend itself poses: is a doubling in thirty days a repricing based on new information, or momentum pricing? Nothing in this evidence names a new customer, a shipped volume, or a revenue figure.
- Say the sourcing on the card: two links, one of them the company's own page. Treat every claim about performance as Etched's claim until an independent benchmark exists.

## The Catch (mandatory skeptic beat)
A valuation is what one set of buyers agreed to pay, not what a business is worth and not what it has earned. There is no revenue in this evidence, no disclosed customer, no independent benchmark and no shipping volume. And the specialisation that justifies the price is also the single biggest risk the company carries: a chip tuned for one architecture is a bet that the architecture stays put, in a field that has changed its mind roughly every eighteen months.
