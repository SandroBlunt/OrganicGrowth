# LG and Nvidia are putting humanoid AI robots into appliance factories next year

## Suggested Recipe
News Carousel & News Short Script

## Angle
LG partnered with Nvidia to build manufacturing humanoid robots using Nvidia's Isaac GR00T foundation model, aiming for real factory floor deployment in 2027.

## Hook Concept
Address the gap between viral CGI robot demos and the actual engineering reality of manufacturing washing machines and refrigerators.

## Talking Points
- LG and Nvidia signed an agreement on August 13 to co-develop industrial humanoid robots powered by the Isaac GR00T foundation model.
- The partnership targets physical logistics, assembly, and parts handling across LG's global smart factory network.
- A public prototype demonstration is scheduled for Q1 2027 ahead of staged manufacturing trials.
- The honest catch: humanoid form factors remain significantly more expensive and mechanically failure-prone than existing wheeled automated guided vehicles (AGVs).
