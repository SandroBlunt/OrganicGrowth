# Amazon quietly trained AI on years of Twitch streams by opting everyone in by default

## Suggested Recipe
News Carousel & News Short Script

## Angle
Twitch creators discovered that Amazon has been using their live streams, voices, and chat logs to train generative AI models by turning opt-in on by default for all accounts.

## Hook Concept
Open with the realization that every hour of gaming or talking on Twitch was silently converted into training data for Amazon's models.

## Talking Points
- Twitch confirmed that creator video and audio streams have been ingested by parent company Amazon for generative AI training.
- The policy was enabled as a default setting for all creators, sparking intense community pushback across social media.
- Twitch subsequently added a settings toggle allowing creators to opt out of future model training runs.
- The honest catch: flipping the toggle to 'opt out' only prevents future scraping; data already absorbed into existing trained neural network weights cannot be deleted.
