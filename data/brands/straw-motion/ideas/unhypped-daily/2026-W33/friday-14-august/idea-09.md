# Microsoft is building its own AI models again — and its image generator just hit #2 on Arena

## Suggested Recipe
News Carousel & News Short Script

## Angle
Microsoft AI launched its in-house MAI-Thinking-1 reasoning model and MAI-Image-2.6 generator, signaling that Microsoft is actively building internal alternatives to OpenAI's stack.

## Hook Concept
Note the irony of Microsoft funding OpenAI while quietly building models that beat Google, Meta, and xAI on public leaderboards.

## Talking Points
- Microsoft AI announced MAI-Thinking-1, its first proprietary reasoning model utilizing internal chain-of-thought verification.
- MAI-Image-2.6 debuted at #2 on the Artificial Analysis Arena leaderboard, beating image models from Google, Meta, and xAI.
- The models represent Microsoft AI's push under Mustafa Suleyman to establish first-party AI IP rather than relying exclusively on partner APIs.
- The honest catch: dual-tracking in-house MAI models alongside OpenAI integrations creates duplicate tooling and confusing product tiers for enterprise Azure clients.
