# Claude in Chrome turns into an active coworker that reads across your tabs

## Suggested Recipe
News Carousel & News Short Script

## Angle
Anthropic turned Claude's Chrome side panel from a passive Q&A assistant into an active coworker that inspects open browser tabs and carries context between web apps.

## Hook Concept
Highlight the shift from copying and pasting text into Claude to letting Claude look directly at your open browser tabs.

## Talking Points
- Anthropic upgraded its Chrome extension into a full Cowork session, enabling continuous ambient assistance across browsing sessions.
- Claude can now inspect DOM elements, extract tabular data from web pages, and cross-reference information between open tabs without manual copy-paste.
- The workflow allows users to delegate research synthesis and multi-step web navigation while continuing normal work in another window.
- The honest catch: giving an AI agent active visibility across browser tabs exposes sensitive session tokens, internal company dashboards, and private web data.
