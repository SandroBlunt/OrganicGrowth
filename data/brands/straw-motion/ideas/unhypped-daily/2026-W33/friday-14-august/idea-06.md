# Liquid AI fit an on-device screen reader and tool caller into just 3 gigabytes

## Suggested Recipe
News Carousel & News Short Script

## Angle
Liquid AI launched a 3.1B parameter non-transformer vision model that fits inside 3GB of RAM and achieves high accuracy on UI screen parsing and local tool execution.

## Hook Concept
Show that you don't need a cloud server or an Nvidia datacenter GPU to let an AI look at your laptop screen and click buttons.

## Talking Points
- Liquid AI released LFM2.5-VL-3B, a 3.1-billion parameter vision-language model designed for on-device local deployment in ~3 GB of memory.
- The model scored 80.7 on ScreenSpot-v2 and improved visual grounding on RefCOCO from 57.1 to 87.9.
- Native function calling is included, with ToolSandbox benchmark performance jumping from 26.4 to 59.5.
- The honest catch: 3B models are specialized visual operators; they excel at clicking buttons and reading UI, but lack the broad general reasoning needed for ambiguous multi-step workflows.
