# Script v1 — FIRESHIP STYLE
**Straw Motion · "AI's Week, Unhypped"** — read aloud, ~6–7 min.
Voice: cold hook, fast, dry wit, self-posed pivots. Each story ends on **what you can do with it** (business owner / marketer / AI-career), not a skeptic beat. Shots → `shot-list.md`.

---

**[SHOT 1]** Seven days. Four new ways to make video out of thin air — and one of them turns your sales deck into a movie. This was the week AI-generated media stopped being a demo and became a tool you can pick up. Everything that shipped, and exactly what you do with each one.

This is Straw Motion. Unhypped.

**[SHOT 2]** We start in China. Alibaba dropped **Wan 3.0**. The headline trick: **[SHOT 3]** you feed it a document — a PDF, a spreadsheet, a slide deck, even a web link — and it hands you back a video. Thirty seconds, sound included, up to 1080p, about six bucks a clip.
**What you do with it:** if you run a business or do marketing, this is "turn the case study you already wrote into a promo clip" — no crew, no editor. Building an AI career? "Documents in, video out" is the exact service clients will pay for next quarter. *(One honesty note: I could only confirm the specs from a secondary source — check Alibaba's own post before you quote numbers.)*

**[SHOT 4]** ByteDance shipped **Seedance 2.5**. Thirty-second clips — double the last version — with audio baked in and fifty-plus reference images, so **[SHOT 5]** your character keeps the same face across every shot. That part is the whole game.
**What you do with it:** **[SHOT 6]** short product ads where your mascot, your founder, or your product looks identical in every cut. For a marketer, consistent-character video is the thing clients have been begging for.

**[SHOT 7]** **MiniMax H3** makes the picture and the sound in one pass — video and stereo audio together. It runs through ComfyUI, so it's a builder's tool, not a one-click app.
**What you do with it:** if you want to sell AI video as a service, this is the open kit to learn now — get good at this pipeline and you're ahead of everyone waiting for the polished app.

**[SHOT 8]** And the cleanest results of the bunch: **FLUX 3** from Black Forest Labs. **[SHOT 9]** One model — images, video, and native audio, all from the same brain. **[SHOT 10]** There's even a robotics spin-off that reacts in 101 milliseconds.
**What you do with it:** one tool for your whole content stack — the thumbnail, the clip, and the voiceover from a single model. Learn one thing, cover three jobs.

So that's four video models in a week, all merging sound and picture into one step. If your content still treats them separately, that's your upgrade this month.

**[SHOT 11]** Quick jump to still images: Microsoft's own generator, **MAI-Image-2.6**, landed at number two on the public leaderboard.
**What you do with it:** more strong image tools means your graphics get cheaper — and this one lives right inside the Microsoft stack a lot of businesses already pay for. Competition is your discount.

**[SHOT 12]** Headphones on. Three labs shipped voice AI that answers in 450 milliseconds — human speed, no awkward robot pause.
**What you do with it:** if you take bookings or run support, near-instant voice agents are now realistic — phone lines that actually feel like a conversation. "Voice-agent builder" is a job title people are hiring for right now.

**[SHOT 13]** Two big ones to close. **Meta** gave away a 30-billion-parameter model — free, Apache license — that runs on a single 24GB gaming card.
**What you do with it:** run capable AI in-house, private, with no per-message bill. For a small business that's real money and real data control. For your career, local-model skills are suddenly in demand.

**[SHOT 14]** And Google shipped **Gemini 3.7 Flash** three weeks after 3.6 and cut the price in half.
**What you do with it:** anything you shelved because the AI bill was too high — take it off the shelf. The pattern of the whole week: it keeps getting cheaper, so build now and stay ready to switch.

**[SHOT 15]** That's the week — everything that shipped, and what it's actually good for. If this saved you twenty tabs, subscribe. Every week, minus the hype. See you next drop.
