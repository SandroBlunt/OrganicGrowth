# Script v3 — UNHYPPED NEWS STYLE
**Straw Motion · "AI's Week, Unhypped"** — read aloud, ~6–7 min.
Voice: easy, no-BS, founder-confident, big-picture, direct. Each story ends on **where it fits in your business/career**, not a skeptic beat. Shots → `shot-list.md`.

---

**[SHOT 1]** Four new video models. One that turns a spreadsheet into a film. Prices cut in half. This was the week generative media became something you can put to work. Here's what shipped — and exactly where it fits for you.

Straw Motion. Unhypped. Let's get into it.

**[SHOT 2]** Alibaba shipped **Wan 3.0**. The move that matters: **[SHOT 3]** feed it a document or a URL and it returns a finished video — sound included, thirty seconds, around six dollars a clip.
**Where it fits:** marketing content from assets you already own. Your report, your landing page, your deck — straight to a promo clip, no production team. Building an AI career? "Documents to video" is a service you can sell this quarter. *(Verify the specs against Alibaba's own post — we had one source.)*

**[SHOT 4]** ByteDance countered with **Seedance 2.5**: thirty-second clips, audio included, and **[SHOT 5]** fifty-plus references so a character holds its face across shots.
**Where it fits:** **[SHOT 6]** real ads. Consistent product and brand character across every cut is the difference between a demo and something you'd actually run.

**[SHOT 7]** **MiniMax H3** makes picture and audio in one pass — powerful, but it's a ComfyUI pipeline, a builder's tool.
**Where it fits:** if you're the technical one, this is the open stack to master and offer as a service before it's a polished product.

**[SHOT 8]** **FLUX 3** is the one to watch — **[SHOT 9]** one model for images, video, and audio, **[SHOT 10]** with a robotics arm reacting in 101 milliseconds.
**Where it fits:** your whole content pipeline on a single model. That's a platform decision, not a one-off tool.

Step back: four launches, one direction — audio and video merging into a single step. If your workflow still separates them, you're behind the roadmap.

**[SHOT 11]** On images, Microsoft put its own generator, **MAI-Image-2.6**, at number two on the public board.
**Where it fits:** another strong image option, likely cheaper, inside a stack you already pay for. More competition, lower cost on your creative.

**[SHOT 12]** Voice: three labs shipped assistants replying in 450 milliseconds — human speed.
**Where it fits:** support, sales, and booking lines that actually feel human. If you talk to customers by phone, this is the year it becomes real.

**[SHOT 13]** The two that matter most for builders. **Meta** open-weighted a capable 30-billion-parameter model, Apache-licensed, that runs on a single 24GB card.
**Where it fits:** run serious AI in-house — private, no per-call fee. It rewrites your cost structure and your data story overnight.

**[SHOT 14]** And Google shipped **Gemini 3.7 Flash** at half the price of three weeks ago.
**Where it fits:** revisit anything you killed for being too expensive. The through-line of the week — capability is getting cheap, so build lean and stay ready to switch.

**[SHOT 15]** The week in one line: media generation is consolidating, compute is getting cheap — and all of it is ready for you to use. That's the week, unhypped. Subscribe for the signal without the noise. See you next drop.
